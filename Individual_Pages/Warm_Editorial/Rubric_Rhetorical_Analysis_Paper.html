Grading Rubric · Week 9

Rhetorical Analysis Paper (64 pts)

A 3-4 page Aristotelian critique of a contemporary persuasive speech, demonstrating mastery of Ethos, Pathos, and Logos as analytical lenses rather than buzzwords.

Scoring Criteria

CriterionPointsExcellence Standard
Thesis & speech selection10A specific persuasive speech is selected and a defensible analytical thesis articulates the speech's rhetorical strategy.
Ethos analysis12Paper examines how the speaker constructs credibility, authority, and goodwill with the audience.
Pathos analysis12Paper analyzes the emotional appeals — imagery, narrative, values — and their intended audience effect.
Logos analysis12Paper dissects the reasoning, evidence, and argumentative structure underpinning the speech.
Organization & textual support10Claims are supported with direct quotations and specific moments from the speech, organized into coherent paragraphs.
Mechanics & citation8Writing is polished, largely error-free, and the speech is properly cited in MLA or APA format.
Total64This category contributes 64 points to the 800-point course total.
