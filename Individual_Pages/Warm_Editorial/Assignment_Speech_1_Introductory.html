Major Speech Assignment

Speech 1: Introductory Speech (100 pts)

⏱️ Speech Duration Limit

2–3 minutes

📑 Outline & Note Card Rules

One keyword note card allowed (max 50 words total). No full outline permitted on stage. Full preparation outline submitted online beforehand (30 pts).

📌 Speech Presentation Instructions

Introduce a fellow classmate or a significant personal artifact to the audience. This speech serves to establish your baseline extemporaneous delivery style, focus on eye contact, somatic posture, and basic structural introduction, body, and conclusion.

📹 Recording & Camera Framing Regulations

Frame yourself waist-up so hand gestures are visible. Stand in an acoustically balanced environment facing your light source directly to avoid silhouettes.
