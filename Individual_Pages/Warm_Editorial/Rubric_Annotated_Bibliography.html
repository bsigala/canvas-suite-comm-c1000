Grading Rubric · Week 5

Annotated Bibliography (32 pts)

Four credible sources are gathered, formatted, summarized, and evaluated in support of the Speech 2 informative topic. This is the research foundation for verbal citations.

Scoring Criteria

CriterionPointsExcellence Standard
Source quantity & credibility8Exactly four sources are provided, drawn from peer-reviewed academic journals, books, or authoritative government reports.
Citation formatting8Each source is cited in a consistent academic format (MLA or APA) with complete bibliographic information.
Summary annotation8Each annotation concisely summarizes the source's main argument and key evidence in the student's own words.
Evaluation & relevance8Each annotation assesses the source's credibility, currency, and direct relevance to the Speech 2 topic.
Total32This category contributes 32 points to the 800-point course total.
