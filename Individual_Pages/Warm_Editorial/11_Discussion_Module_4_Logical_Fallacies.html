Discussion Assignment

Module 4 Discussion: Rhetorical Fallacy Spotting (Week 11)

📌 Discussion Prompt Instructions

Find a commercial or political speech clip, identify a logical fallacy (Ad Hominem, False Dilemma, Slippery Slope, Straw Man), explain the violation, and rewrite it soundly.

👥 Substantive Peer Collaboration Requirements

Mandatory Replies: Assess fallacy identification with peers and evaluate alternative interpretations of the speech clip. Initial post must be at least 250 words; peer responses must be at least 100 words each. Adhere to the Discussion Etiquette guidelines.
