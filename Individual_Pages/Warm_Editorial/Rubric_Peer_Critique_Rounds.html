Peer Critique Rounds — 3 Rounds (72 pts total)

Three standardized rounds of rubric-aligned peer evaluation (24 points each) following Speech 1, Speech 2, and Speech 3. Students learn to give feedback that is specific, kind, and actionable.

Scoring Criteria

CriterionPointsExcellence Standard
Substantive, specific feedback (per round)10Feedback names concrete moments (delivery, structure, citations) rather than vague praise, and offers at least one actionable improvement.

Timely completion & etiquette (per round)6Critique is submitted by the deadline and follows Discussion Etiquette: respect, constructiveness, and a growth mindset.
