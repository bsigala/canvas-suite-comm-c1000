About This Course

In COMM C1000 Introduction to Public Speaking (formerly SPCH 101), students learn and apply foundational rhetorical theories and techniques of public speaking in a multicultural democratic society. Students discover, develop, and critically analyze ideas in public discourse through research, reasoning, organization, composition, delivery, and evaluation of various types of speeches, including informative and persuasive speeches.
