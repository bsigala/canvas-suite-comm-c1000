COMM C1000 Intro to Public Speaking

Formerly SPCH 101 · Fall 2026 Opening Announcement

Hello everyone! My name is Benjamin Sigala, and welcome to COMM C1000 Introduction to Public Speaking (formerly SPCH 101)! In this course, you will learn and apply foundational rhetorical theories and techniques of public speaking in a multicultural democratic society. You will discover, develop, and critically analyze ideas in public discourse through research, reasoning, organization, composition, delivery, and evaluation of various types of speeches, including informative and persuasive speeches. This course is designed for Communication Studies majors and recommended for students interested in the Forensics: Speech and Debate team, and/or Pathway to Law.

This class is designed as an **entirely asynchronous online (WEB) course**, meaning we don't meet live on campus, but we still operate as a synchronized team! **You will complete and submit weekly assignments in this online course through Canvas.**

Required Materials

Expect to spend about 15 hours a week on reading, outlining, recording, and peer reviewing. To keep this course highly accessible, **our textbook is entirely digital and completely free!** You can access it directly inside our modules or via the link in our course syllabus.

What to Expect from the Class

  • My philosophy is that education should be fun! I'm a "learn by doing" style educator, so you will complete multiple small tasks weekly to get a feel for the speaking skills we are developing, before compiling them into a final speech.
  • As long as you follow directions and submit on time, you will earn full credit for small homework check-ins. However, I will critically evaluate the larger outlines, visual aids, and speech videos with structured rubrics, so you can expect to see my feedback in your Grades page.

Completing Work & Participation

  • Strict Deadlines: Any assignments submitted after their scheduled Canvas deadlines will receive a score of zero. Modules and tasks are unlocked on a weekly pace to help you establish consistent, effective online learning habits.
  • Drop Policy: Consistent submission is how I track attendance. Students who miss two consecutive weeks of work will be dropped from the course.
