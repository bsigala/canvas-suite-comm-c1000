To kick off our public speaking journey, you are going to record and post a short, friendly video introducing yourself. You will also view and reply to your peers. This helps us overcome initial speech anxieties and learn a bit about each other!

Part 1: Your Icebreaker Video (10 Points)

Due Thursday, August 20, 2026 @ 11:59 PM

Record a 2 to 5 minute video introducing yourself. In your recording, please address the following questions:

  1. What is your name?
  2. What is your major and educational goals?
  3. What is one piece of advice you would give to your younger self?
  4. Lastly, share at least one thing in your life for which you are grateful. (There is no right or wrong answer, just an opportunity to share)

Recording Options: You can record your video right inside Canvas by hitting the "Reply" button below, and choosing the "Play" icon above the text input. If you experience technical errors, feel free to upload a pre-recorded file from your phone, attach an iCloud photo link, or insert a YouTube upload link.

Part 2: Peer Replies (10 Points)

Due Sunday, August 23, 2026 @ 11:59 PM

Once you've posted your video, view your classmates' recordings and respond to at least TWO of them (each reply is worth 5 points). Your responses must comply with the following standards:

  • Each reply must be at least two sentences long.
  • Talk about something you have in common, offer an encouraging word, or ask a thoughtful follow-up question related to their goals or gratitude statements.
  • Your replies must remain respectful, civil, and completely aligned with our Course Netiquette Rules.
  • Please space your replies throughout the discussion period rather than posting them all on the same day. This encourages natural, organic dialogue!

Total possible points: 20 Points (Part 1 = 10pts | Part 2 = 10pts)
