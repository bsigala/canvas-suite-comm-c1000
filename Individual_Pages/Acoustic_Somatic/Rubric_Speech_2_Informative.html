Grading Rubric · Speech 2

Speech 2: Informative Speech (100 pts)

A 4-5 minute research-backed informative speech with a visual aid deck, teaching the audience something genuinely new through clear explanation and verbally cited evidence.

Scoring Criteria

CriterionPointsExcellence Standard
Topic clarity & audience relevance10Topic is well-scoped, inherently interesting, and adapted to the audience's knowledge level.
Research depth (4 sources)20Speech draws on at least four credible sources with substantive, accurate, and current supporting evidence.
Organization & transitions20Information is logically sequenced (e.g., chronological, topical, or spatial) with clear signposts and transitions.
Verbal citations20All sources are cited aloud with author credibility, publication title, and date woven naturally into delivery.
Visual aid quality15Slides are high-contrast, low-text-density, and genuinely reinforce rather than duplicate the spoken content.
Delivery15Delivery is extemporaneous, engaging, and appropriately paced for audience comprehension.
Total100This category contributes 100 points to the 800-point course total.
