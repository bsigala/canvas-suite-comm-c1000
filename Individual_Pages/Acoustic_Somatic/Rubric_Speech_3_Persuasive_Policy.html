Grading Rubric · Speech 3

Speech 3: Persuasive Policy Speech (150 pts)

The capstone persuasive performance: a 5-7 minute policy speech structured strictly on Monroe's Motivated Sequence, backed by five verbally cited sources, and free of logical fallacies.

Scoring Criteria

CriterionPointsExcellence Standard
Monroe's Motivated Sequence structure30All five steps — Attention, Need, Satisfaction, Visualization, Action — are present, distinct, and correctly sequenced.
Argument quality & evidence (5 sources)30The policy claim is supported by five credible, current sources with rigorous, relevant evidence.
Ethos / Pathos / Logos balance20Credibility, emotional resonance, and logical reasoning are deliberately balanced for maximum persuasive effect.
Fallacy-free reasoning15Argument contains no Ad Hominem, Straw Man, False Dilemma, Slippery Slope, or other logical fallacies.
Verbal citations20All five sources are cited aloud with author, publication, and date, integrated smoothly into the persuasive flow.
Delivery & persuasive conviction20Delivery conveys genuine conviction through vocal variety, confident posture, and purposeful gestures.
Clarity of call to action15The Action step issues a specific, achievable, and frictionless call to action the audience can act on immediately.
Total150This category contributes 150 points to the 800-point course total.
