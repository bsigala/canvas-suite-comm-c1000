🎙️ Course Description & Acoustic Geometry Framework

Welcome to COMM C1000: Introduction to Public Speaking! This in-person course provides an immersive, hands-on studio environment for mastering the art, science, and somatic experience of public discourse. Grounded in C-ID COMM 110 requirements, CAL-GETC Area 1C standards, and our proprietary Acoustic Geometry & Somatic Speech Analysis Framework, you will learn to transmute speech anxiety into vocal resonance, design logically airtight persuasive arguments, conduct rigorous audience analysis, and deliver authoritative presentations before a live classroom audience.

📖 Zero Cost Textbook (ZTC) & Open Educational Resources (OER): All course readings are 100% free and provided directly inside Canvas. You do NOT need to buy a textbook! We utilize Stand up, Speak out: The Practice and Ethics of Public Speaking alongside curated media guides.

🎤 In-Person Presentation Studio & Attendance Protocols

1. Live Stage Speech Days

All major speeches are delivered live in-person on scheduled presentation days before your peers. Attendance is mandatory for both your presentation day and as an active audience member supporting your classmates.

2. Attendance & Participation

Class begins promptly on time. Active listening, peer evaluations, and workshop exercises form a vital component of your 800-point course structure. Arriving late on speech days disrupts speakers and impacts participation credit.

3. Optional Video Recording Policy

Practice sessions and speeches may be recorded for private self-reflection and instructor feedback. Recordings are strictly optional, non-public, and used solely for student growth. Students may opt out at any time without penalty.

🧪 Reverse-Engineered High-Density Quiz Architecture (Mastery Model)

Our quizzes are not simple recall tests. They are dense, scenario-driven cognitive benchmarks engineered through Bloom's Taxonomy to evaluate applied communication theory, critical fallacy detection, and acoustic mechanics.

BLOOM'S LEVEL III-V Scenario & Applied Analysis

Questions present real-world speaking dilemmas, asking you to diagnose audience dynamics, logical fallacies, and vocal modulation.

CUMULATIVE MASTERY Unlimited Retakes to 100%

Attempts build upon prior feedback. Correct responses form your foundation; retakes target gaps until total concept mastery is achieved.

DISTRACTOR RIGOR High-Plausibility Options

Distractors reflect common public speaking misconceptions, requiring keen discernment rather than elimination guesses.

SCAVENGER HUNT LAB 100-Point Synthesis Project

An innovative multi-chapter synthesis project where you construct and analyze high-caliber test questions across Ch 1–4.
